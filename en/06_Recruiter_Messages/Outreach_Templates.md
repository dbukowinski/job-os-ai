# Recruiter messages — templates

## First message
Hi [Name], I came across the [Role] opening at [Company]. The [specific scope] caught my attention in particular. I worked on [confirmed project/skill], so I see a good fit. If the process is still open, I would be glad to have a short conversation.

## Follow-up
Hi [Name], a quick follow-up on my application for [Role] from [date]. The role is still very interesting to me, especially [specific]. Could you let me know whether the process is still open? Thanks!

## Cold outreach
Hi [Name], I spotted your [Role] opening. The [specific aspect] caught my attention. My experience with [real relevant experience] seems aligned, so I'd be happy to compare notes if the role is still open.

Before sending, check the company, role, context, link and the candidate's approval.
