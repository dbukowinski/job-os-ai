# Job search policy

## Rules

1. Read `Job Tracker` and `Offer Memory` before a scan; without reading them, never publish a definite "new" offer.
2. Preferred sources: company pages and the target ATS first, job boards/aggregators later.
3. Freshness: start with ads from the last 24–72 hours. Older ones have already collected many applications.
4. Pick two main sources and check them daily instead of sending the same CV to five places at once.
5. Record every offer you notice in Offer Memory, including rejected, closed and talent-pool ones.
6. Recognise exact / likely / semantic duplicates. When in doubt, hold the alert.
7. Confirm the active form and the country, hours and pay conditions (if available).
8. Analyse the main career track and side gigs separately; do not mix full-time with 10–20 h/week.
9. Report the direct link, fit, constraints and how well the data is confirmed.
10. Write to Memory and verify by reading back before notifying.
11. Once a week, backfill older active ads and audit false alerts.

## Job sources (as of 24.11.2026 — review every quarter)

A starter list for Poland, where the system was built. Replace or extend it for your country and role in `Candidate_Profile_TEMPLATE.yaml`.

| Source | What for | Tactic |
|---|---|---|
| Company career pages and their ATS (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Teamtailor) | The original form, certain activity | Apply here even if you found the ad on an aggregator |
| LinkedIn Jobs | Large base of ads, often shows the recruiter | Date posted filter: past 24 hours |
| Pracuj.pl | General Polish job board, all industries | Save the search with alerts |
| No Fluff Jobs | IT plus marketing, sales, finance, HR (Poland and nearby countries) | Requires a salary range in every ad — compare ranges across several ads before sending your first application |
| JustJoin.it, theprotocol.it | IT jobs in Poland | Filter by seniority and work mode |
| Indeed | Aggregator of many sources | Job title in quotes = exact phrase; check activity at the source |
| Useme | Freelance jobs and invoicing in Poland | Side-gig track; check scope and deadline |
| Upwork | International freelance jobs | Before a contract, contact only through the platform — use the contact-free CV variant |
| Wellfound | Startups, often with salary and equity | Start from company stage, not job title |
| Remote OK | Remote work, public pay | Compare ranges for the same role |
| Glassdoor (Interviews) | Reports on hiring processes | Only entries from the last 12 months; into `Company_Process_TEMPLATE.md` |

## Records and control

- Every offer in Offer Memory has a source (column P) — after a few weeks you can see which source leads to interviews.
- Replace a source that has produced no reply for 4 weeks.
