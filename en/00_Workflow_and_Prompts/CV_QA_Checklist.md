# CV — content, ATS, Google Docs and PDF checklist

## Facts
- [ ] First and last name are correct, including diacritics.
- [ ] Dates, titles, companies, achievements and tools come from the Master CV / Career Knowledge Base.
- [ ] No invented numbers, projects, certificates or technologies.
- [ ] Keywords from the ad have status ✅ in `Market_Requirements.md`; no ❌ requirement is listed as a skill.
- [ ] CV = a separate document; the Master CV is unchanged.

## Layout (ATS)
- [ ] One column; no tables, icons, graphics, sidebars.
- [ ] Aptos 10.5 base, sections 12–14 bold, readable line spacing.
- [ ] The whole header is centred.
- [ ] All section headings are bold.
- [ ] Role headings are bold in full: ROLE | COMPANY — project/context.
- [ ] Skills as short phrases separated by commas or semicolons; no long lists with `|`.
- [ ] LinkedIn and portfolio/GitHub, if given, are live links.
- [ ] GDPR/privacy clause adapted to the country and requirements; small light-grey text at the end, no heading.

## Google Docs (known pitfalls)
- [ ] Styles applied after the last text change (replacing text can drop bold from the last letter).
- [ ] The last character of every section and role heading is bold (e.g. a final "S" or ")").
- [ ] No empty paragraphs used as spacing; each role heading stays with its dates and bullets.
- [ ] No other paragraph is accidentally bold.

## PDF (what the recruiter sees decides)
- [ ] PDF exported after the last edit.
- [ ] At most 2 pages; no empty extra page.
- [ ] Both pages viewed at normal zoom: line wraps, no hanging separators, spacing, centred header.
- [ ] Text in the PDF can be selected and copied (the ATS can read it).
- [ ] The clause appears only at the bottom of the last page.

## Contact-free variant (freelance platforms)
- [ ] For platforms that forbid off-platform contact before a contract (e.g. Upwork): no email, phone or contact links in the text or the PDF.
- [ ] A normal CV (outside such platforms) has the full contact block.

## Tracker
- [ ] The `CV version` column links to the latest checked PDF / Google Doc.
- [ ] Older versions marked as superseded or moved to `08_Archive/`.
- [ ] You say "done" only after exporting and looking at the PDF.

Sample English clause (use only if appropriate; this is not legal advice):

I hereby consent to the processing of my personal data contained in this document by current and future employers for the purposes necessary to carry out current and future recruitment processes, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (GDPR).
