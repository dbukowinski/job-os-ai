# Mock interviews — log

One mock interview a week, at a fixed time in your calendar, about 60 minutes — until the real interview, not one the day before.

**How:**
- With the AI assistant (prompt 16.5 in the playbook), with another person or on a mock-interview service.
- If you can, record it and listen the next day. That is usually when you hear that you think in silence instead of saying what you are doing, or that you talk too long.
- After each one, pick **one** thing to improve next time.
